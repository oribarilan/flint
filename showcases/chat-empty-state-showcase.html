NOTES

  • Container: 1032×520px overlay, 40/60 split. Chat panel is ~619px wide.
  • Interaction: Clicking a chip fills the input field (simulated). In production, it would send immediately or fill-and-focus.
  • Fidelity gap: DM Sans font is not loaded in the showcase — using system sans-serif fallback. Production uses bundled DM Sans.
  • Fidelity gap: Emoji icons are used in place of the actual SVG icon system.
  • Theme: Toggle between dark (default) and light. Both use exact spec token values.
  • A (Whisper) — Least visual weight. Lets the input placeholder and attention panel do the talking. Risk: may feel empty/broken to new users.
  • B (Spark) — Safe default. Clear affordance. Risk: generic.
  • C (Guide) — Most discoverable. Descriptions explain what each action does. Risk: heavy, may scroll on shorter overlays, repeats information visible in the attention panel.
  • D (Strike) — Most personality. Short chip labels suggest action, not question. Keyboard hint reinforces power-user identity. Risk: too opinionated, greeting may feel forced.
