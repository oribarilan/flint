How this works: macOS Tray.setTitle() renders plain text next to the tray icon in the system menu bar. Text is rendered in the system font (SF Pro) at the system's menubar size. The text cannot be styled (no bold, color, etc.) — visual weight and dots shown above are simulated to explore options. In the real implementation, styles marked "rich" would need ANSI-attributed strings or may be limited to plain text only.

Constraints: Menubar space is shared with other apps. Wider text pushes system icons left. Aim for ≤20 chars. On smaller screens or laptops with notch, space is even tighter.
